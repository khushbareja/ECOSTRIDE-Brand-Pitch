# 👟🌿 ECOSTRIDE: AI-Powered Sustainable Footwear Strategy

Welcome to the repository for ECOSTRIDE, a fictional sustainable sneaker brand and AI-powered content strategy pitched for Indian Gen Z and urban youth. This project was developed as Academic Task 1 for the Prompt Engineering for Generative AI (MGN 255) course at Lovely Professional University.

## 🌍 Project Overview & Mission

The brand's mission is to make everyday sneakers more conscious without complicating style, operating under the tagline "Step Forward. Leave Less Behind." We address the problem of fast fashion waste, specifically the 20 billion pairs of synthetic shoes produced globally each year and petroleum-based EVAs that take over 300 years to decompose.

* 🧠 **Core Philosophy:** We prioritize material consciousness, radical transparency, everyday comfort, modern design, and responsible innovation.


* 🎯 **Target Audience:** Designed for college students who seek trendy everyday wear, and young professionals who value minimalism and transparency.


* 👟 **Product Lines:** The collection features the minimalist ECOSTRIDE CORE, the active ECOSTRIDE PULSE, the smart-casual ECOSTRIDE EARTH, and the fully recyclable ECOSTRIDE LOOP.


* 🌾 **Material Architecture:** Shoes are engineered using organic cotton, recycled polyester, natural rubber, bio-based EVA, bio-canvas leather alternatives, and a unique rice-waste agricultural composite from North India.



## 🤖⚙️ AI Prompt Engineering Strategy

This project relies heavily on structured AI prompt engineering to generate scalable social marketing assets, moving away from generic prompts to those utilizing specific roles, constraints, and negative filters.

* 💬 **ChatGPT (GPT-40):** Utilized for content calendars, SEO keyword analysis, and Instagram captions using role-based prompting, negative constraints, and few-shot examples.


* 📸 **Midjourney v6 / DALL-E 3:** Employed to generate photorealistic sneaker product concepts using art director personas, specific camera angles, and lighting parameters.


* 🎬 **Runway Gen-2 / Luma Dream Machine:** Used for short-form 9:16 vertical video Reels via temporal scene breakdowns and motion prompts.


* 🖌️ **Canva AI:** Leveraged for presentation layout and visual hierarchy structuring.
